What else matters?

Condition, buyer financing, permits, contractor availability, market timing and cash needed upfront can make an as-is sale more practical even when a renovation scenario produces a higher modeled net. For estate or divorce property, obtain necessary approvals before authorizing work.

Should I fix my house before selling?

Use this sell as-is vs renovate calculator to compare estimated proceeds after renovation costs, contingencies, carrying expenses and selling costs. A higher renovated sale price does not always mean a higher net result.

If you're deciding whether to sell an inherited house as-is or renovate a home before listing, consider project delays, contractor availability, unexpected repairs and local buyer demand. A local comparative market analysis can help test the sale price assumptions.
