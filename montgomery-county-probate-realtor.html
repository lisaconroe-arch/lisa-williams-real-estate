A practical roadmap

What selling an estate property can look like

1. Start with the property. We discuss the home, current condition, occupancy, known issues and the family's goals.
2. Confirm who has authority to act. The appropriate attorney and title professionals can determine what documentation or authority is required for the estate.
3. Review market value and selling options. I analyze relevant market information and discuss potential as-is and prepared-for-market strategies.
4. Prepare the property for the chosen strategy. This may include belongings, cleaning, repairs, contractor access, photographs and showing preparation.
5. Market and negotiate the property. Once listed, I manage the real estate marketing, showing activity, offers and negotiations.
6. Coordinate through closing. I stay involved with the real estate side of the transaction while title and legal professionals handle matters within their respective roles.

Frequently asked questions

Montgomery County probate real estate questions

Do I need to wait until probate is finished before talking to a REALTOR®?

No. You can begin gathering property and market information before you are ready to list. Your attorney and title professionals should advise you regarding legal authority and when the estate can enter into a transaction.

Can an inherited house be sold as-is?

Depending on the property and circumstances, an as-is marketing strategy may be an option. I can help evaluate the real estate and market considerations so the seller can compare that approach with making improvements.

Should we renovate an inherited home before selling it?

Not automatically. The potential return should be compared with the cost, time and risk of the work. A market assessment can help identify whether improvements are likely to make sense.

What if several heirs are involved?

Multiple heirs can make communication especially important. Legal questions about ownership, authority, consent or the estate should be directed to the appropriate attorney or title professional. I can coordinate the real estate side with the authorized decision-makers.

What if the house is full of belongings?

You can still contact me before everything is removed. We can first evaluate the property and determine what preparation is actually important for the chosen sale strategy.

Can you work with our probate attorney?

Yes. With the client's authorization, I can communicate and coordinate with attorneys, title professionals and other appropriate parties regarding the real estate transaction while respecting each professional's role.
