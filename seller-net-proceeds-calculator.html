How to use your estimate

Start with a realistic potential sale price, request an accurate payoff from your lender, and verify closing expenses with the title company. In probate and divorce matters, legal authority and the distribution of sale proceeds must be confirmed separately.

How much equity will I get selling my house?

This Texas home seller net proceeds calculator estimates what may remain after your mortgage payoff, negotiated brokerage compensation, closing costs, concessions, liens and other entered expenses. It is also useful as a Texas seller closing costs calculator when comparing different sale prices.

Equity and net proceeds are not the same: equity is generally the home's value minus secured debt, while estimated net proceeds also account for selling expenses. Actual title charges, taxes, payoff figures and other closing items can change your final amount.
