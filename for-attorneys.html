Legal authority + market execution

Different responsibilities. One coordinated transaction.

The attorney and REALTOR® serve different professional functions. I do not interpret wills, divorce decrees, court orders or determine who has legal authority to convey property. Instead, I use the legal framework established by counsel and the title company to carry out the real estate side of the transaction.

The attorney's lane

Counsel may advise on legal capacity and authority, probate administration, court orders, settlement terms, ownership disputes, liens or other legal issues affecting the transaction. In a divorce matter, counsel advises the parties regarding their rights, obligations and division of proceeds.

My real estate lane

I evaluate the property's market position, prepare a comparative market analysis, discuss condition and preparation, develop the listing strategy, market the home, manage showings, present offers and coordinate the contract-to-closing process from the real estate side.

When a real estate question becomes a legal question, I send it back to counsel.
Authority, ownership, court orders, probate administration, legal rights and settlement interpretation belong with the attorney. That boundary helps me stay focused on what you referred me to do: handle the property and the real estate transaction professionally.
Divorce-related property

Keeping the property transaction focused when the personal situation is difficult.

A divorce-related sale may require coordination among owners, attorneys, the title company and other professionals. My goal is to keep real estate communication factual, organized and centered on the property.

Communication

I establish a clear communication process consistent with the representation arrangement and the instructions that apply to the transaction. Material real estate information is documented and communicated appropriately rather than allowing the listing to become another source of conflict.

When the parties disagree

I can provide market data and explain the real estate consequences of decisions about pricing, repairs, staging or offer terms. I do not resolve legal disputes between the parties; those issues go back to counsel or the applicable legal process.

Closing & proceeds

I coordinate the real estate closing with the title company. Counsel and the title company address legal instructions affecting ownership, disbursement, escrow or division of proceeds as applicable to the particular matter.

Probate & inherited property

Making sure the listing strategy follows the estate's legal authority.

Authority first

Before relying on an executor, administrator or heir to convey property, the appropriate probate documents, title requirements and legal authority need to be established. I coordinate with counsel and title rather than assuming authority based on a family relationship alone.

Administration matters

Independent and dependent administrations can involve different levels of court supervision. Counsel determines the legal requirements; I build the listing and transaction timeline around those requirements.

Families need coordination

Inherited homes can involve multiple family members, belongings, deferred maintenance and different expectations. I help organize the property-facing decisions while counsel handles legal questions among heirs, beneficiaries or fiduciaries.

Who I work alongside

Built for the different needs of probate, estate and family-law counsel.

The real estate issues may overlap, but the legal matters surrounding each client can be very different. I keep my role focused on the property while working within the framework established by the attorney and title professionals.

Probate Attorneys

When an estate includes real property, I can provide market information, evaluate property condition and help prepare the home for sale while counsel handles appointment, authority, administration and court requirements. Once the appropriate path is established, I carry the real estate transaction forward.

Estate Attorneys

For inherited property, estate planning transitions and other estate-related ownership situations, I can serve as the real estate resource for valuation, sale preparation and market execution. Questions involving ownership, conveyance authority or legal documents stay with counsel and title.

Divorce & Family-Law Attorneys

When the marital home needs to be evaluated or sold, I provide market data and manage the listing process without stepping into the parties' legal dispute. Counsel addresses legal rights, orders, agreements and division issues; I keep the real estate transaction moving.
