A steady real estate process

When emotions are high, the transaction still needs a plan.

Selling a home during divorce can involve decisions about timing, repairs, access for showings, pricing, offers and closing. My role is not to take sides. My role is to represent the real estate transaction professionally, communicate clearly and help keep the property moving forward.

You do not need to have every disagreement resolved before having an initial real estate conversation.

Common questions

Questions homeowners often have about selling during divorce.

Should we repair the house before selling?

Not necessarily. We can compare the likely market benefit of repairs with their cost, time and complexity. In some situations, selling as-is may be the more practical approach.

What if we disagree about the listing?

I can provide real estate information about pricing, preparation and offers, but I do not decide legal disputes between spouses. If an issue depends on a court order, settlement agreement or legal rights, that question belongs with your attorneys.

Can you communicate with both sides?

Communication expectations should be established clearly at the beginning and must be consistent with the representation arrangement and any legal requirements. My goal is to keep real estate communication organized, documented and professional.

Do I need to wait until the divorce is final?

Not every situation follows the same timeline. Whether and when a property can or should be sold depends on the owners, agreements, court orders and circumstances. Your attorney should advise you on the legal timing; I can help plan the real estate timeline around it.
