A coastal-market consideration

Galveston County properties can require extra homework.

For coastal and low-lying properties, buyers may ask about flood history, insurance availability, windstorm coverage, property condition and maintenance. Those factors vary by address; a home on Galveston Island may have different considerations from one farther inland.

Property-specific review

We can discuss known repairs, flood disclosures, condition, occupancy and documents that may help buyers evaluate the home. Insurance questions belong with a licensed insurance professional.

Market strategy

Common questions

Galveston County inherited property FAQs

Can I ask about selling before probate is complete?

Yes. You can discuss market value and preparation early. Your attorney and title company should confirm when and how the property can legally be sold.

Do I need to empty the house before calling?

No. We can first discuss belongings, access and whether cleanup would make sense for the chosen strategy. Follow your attorney's guidance before disposing of estate property.

Does every Galveston County home need flood insurance?

Requirements depend on the property, flood zone, financing and insurance circumstances. A qualified insurance professional and the lender can explain what applies to a particular home.

Can you coordinate with our probate attorney?

Yes, with the client's authorization. I handle real estate marketing and transaction coordination; legal advice and authority questions stay with the attorney.
