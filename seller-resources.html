Before You Make Changes

What Can I Do Now—and What Should I Check First?

📋

Good First Steps

Write down your questions, gather information about the property, ask for a market evaluation, and contact the attorney handling the estate or divorce.

⚖️

Check Before Acting

Before removing or donating belongings, making major repairs, signing a listing agreement, accepting an offer, or deciding how sale money will be divided, confirm who has authority and what approvals are needed.

Common Questions, Simple Answers

Can I clean out an inherited home right away?

Not always. Some belongings may belong to the estate. Ask the estate attorney who can authorize removal, donation, or disposal before you begin.

Can one spouse decide to sell the home?

Not necessarily. Ownership, marital rights, agreements, and court orders can affect who must approve and sign. Check with your divorce attorney and title company.

Do I have to fix everything before selling?

No. We can compare selling as-is with making selected improvements, including costs and likely sale proceeds, before you decide.

Why does the title company need paperwork?

The title company checks ownership, liens, and the documents needed to insure and close the sale. Probate or divorce documents may be required, depending on your situation.

What happens to the money after closing?

It depends on the transaction. Estate funds or divorce sale proceeds may need to be handled under applicable legal requirements, agreements, or court orders. Ask your attorney and title company what applies.
