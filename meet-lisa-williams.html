My story

Real estate guidance with people at the center.

I'm Lisa Williams, a Texas real estate agent with eXp Realty. I work with homeowners and families who want practical answers, a clear selling strategy and someone who will take the time to explain the process.

My work includes traditional residential sales, rental transactions and property situations that can be more complicated—particularly inherited homes, probate-related sales and real estate decisions connected to divorce. These situations may involve multiple decision-makers, tight timelines, property condition concerns and difficult conversations. I focus on the real estate work while respecting the important role of attorneys, title professionals and other advisers.

My background includes an Associate of Arts degree, collegiate honors recognition and experience in community leadership and public relations. I bring that emphasis on communication, organization and service to my real estate practice.
