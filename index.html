How I can help

A Realtor for the real-life part of selling a home.

Probate and divorce sales are still real estate transactions, but they rarely feel like ordinary ones. There may be family decisions, attorneys, deadlines, belongings to sort through, repairs to consider, or a home that hasn't been updated in years. I help you take the real estate side one decision at a time.

Probate & Inherited Homes

I help executors, heirs and families understand the property's current market position, decide whether preparing or selling as-is makes sense, and move from “What do we do with the house?” toward a clear listing and sale plan.

Divorce-Related Home Sales

When a home needs to be sold during a divorce, I focus on the property and the transaction. My goal is straightforward communication, an organized listing process and getting the home from preparation to closing as smoothly as possible.

Traditional Buyers & Sellers

You don't need to be going through probate or divorce to work with me. I also represent buyers and sellers who simply want responsive, hands-on real estate help and someone they can actually reach when questions come up.

“My job is to help you understand what comes next with the property—not make an already stressful situation feel more complicated.”
A more personal approach

You'll work with me, not get passed around.

Real estate can feel especially personal when a home is tied to an estate, a marriage or years of family memories. I believe people deserve honest communication and a Realtor who will answer questions, explain the process in plain language and stay involved.

I can help you look at the home's condition, discuss likely buyer expectations, work through listing preparation, market the property, negotiate offers and coordinate the real estate transaction through closing. When a legal question comes up, I respect that boundary and encourage you to rely on your attorney for legal advice.
