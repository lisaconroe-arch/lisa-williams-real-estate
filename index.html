Real estate resource center

Probate & divorce real estate Q&A

Can an inherited house be sold as-is in Texas?

Often, yes. Whether an as-is strategy makes financial sense depends on the property's condition, buyer pool and the authority of the person selling. Legal questions about an estate should be directed to a Texas attorney.

Should an estate repair a property before listing it?

Not automatically. I compare the likely cost, time and market impact of repairs against an as-is strategy so the decision can be based on the property and local market.

What does a Realtor do during a divorce-related sale?

The Realtor manages the real estate transaction: market analysis, listing preparation, showings, offers and closing coordination. Attorneys advise the parties regarding their legal rights and divorce orders or agreements.

Can an attorney refer a client directly?

Yes. I can speak with the client about the real estate process and keep communication organized, subject to the client's authorization and the needs of the transaction.
