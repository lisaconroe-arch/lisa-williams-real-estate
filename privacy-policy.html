Website Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 8, 2026.

This notice describes how the Texas Probate & Divorce website operated by Lisa Williams, REALTOR®, eXp Realty, LLC, handles information from website visitors. It does not replace the privacy notices of outside services.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use our pages. This may involve cookies and information such as pages viewed, approximate location, browser and device characteristics, referral source, and interactions. We do not intentionally send property addresses, legal case information, or form responses to Google Analytics. You may accept or reject optional analytics using the website banner and change your choice using “Analytics preferences” in the footer. If you reject analytics, the website does not load our Google Analytics script. If you later withdraw consent, the website stops loading analytics on subsequent visits; previously placed Google Analytics cookies may remain until they expire or you clear them in your browser.

Forms and third-party services

If you submit a Free Property Assessment or another inquiry through Jotform, the information you provide is processed to respond to your request. Embedded forms and third-party links may have their own privacy and cookie practices. Calendly, social media platforms and other external services may collect information when you use their services. Please review their policies before sharing sensitive information. Do not submit confidential legal, financial, or family information unless necessary for your inquiry.

Contact

For questions about this website's privacy practices or your inquiry, email Lisa.D.Williams@ExpRealty.com.
