When a home is part of a separation or divorce, deciding what to do next can feel complicated. Frequently asked questions

Montgomery County divorce home sale FAQs

Can I ask for a market assessment before the divorce is final?

Yes. We can discuss the home's market position before a listing decision. Your attorney should advise on legal authority, required consent and any restrictions.

What if one spouse wants to sell and the other does not?

That is a legal matter for the parties' attorneys and, when applicable, the court. I can provide real estate information but cannot resolve a disagreement over legal rights.

Should we repair the house before selling?

Not necessarily. We can compare likely buyer expectations and local sales against the cost, time and inconvenience of improvements.

Can you work with both parties' attorneys?

Yes, when properly authorized and consistent with agency obligations. I communicate on the real estate transaction while each attorney handles their client's legal representation.

How are proceeds divided at closing?

Title and legal professionals follow applicable documents and instructions. I do not determine how proceeds should be divided or provide legal advice.
