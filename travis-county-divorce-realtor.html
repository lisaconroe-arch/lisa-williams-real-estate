What should happen to the house during a divorce?

Deciding whether to sell, retain or prepare a shared home can be one of the most significant property decisions in a divorce. I help Austin and Travis County homeowners understand the real estate side of their options through property-specific market information, thoughtful listing plans and respectful transaction coordination.

Frequently asked questions

Austin divorce real estate FAQs

Can we request a market assessment before a divorce is final?

Yes. You can discuss the home's market position before deciding to list. An attorney should advise on the authority and consent required to sell.

What if one person wants to keep the house?

A property market analysis can help inform the discussion. Lenders and attorneys should address refinancing, ownership and any buyout terms.

What if we disagree about listing or pricing?

Legal disagreements belong with the parties' attorneys or the court. I can provide market information and discuss listing logistics with the authorized decision-makers.

Should we make repairs before selling?

Not automatically. We can compare improvement costs, time and likely buyer expectations with the property's current market position.

Can you coordinate with our attorneys?

Yes, with appropriate authorization and consistent with agency duties. My role is to handle the real estate transaction, not to provide legal advice or determine how proceeds are divided.
