Frequently asked questions

Travis County probate property questions

Can I talk to a REALTOR® before probate is complete?

Yes. You can discuss the property's condition and market before listing. Your attorney and title company should determine when the estate has authority to sell.

Should we remodel an inherited Austin home before selling?

Not automatically. Compare estimated improvement costs, timing and risk with relevant local sales. Sometimes selective work helps; sometimes selling as-is makes more sense.

What if heirs disagree about selling?

Ownership, consent and estate authority are legal questions for an attorney. I can provide property and market information to support conversations with the authorized decision-makers.

Can you coordinate with an Austin probate attorney?

Yes, with the client's permission. I focus on the property's marketing and real estate transaction, while legal professionals advise on probate and authority.
